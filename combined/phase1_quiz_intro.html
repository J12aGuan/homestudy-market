📝 Phase 1 Quiz

This quiz is designed to make sure you understand how Phase 1 of the housing simulation works.

Please answer the following questions before continuing.

💡 Phase 1 Hints

🏠 Property Information:
Each house profile contains information about the home’s structure, layout, and neighborhood attributes.

💲 Hidden Prices:
In Phase 1, market prices are intentionally hidden. You must evaluate houses without knowing their listed price.

📈 Personal Valuation:
For each property, enter the maximum amount you would personally be willing to pay.

🎯 Best Strategy:
Your entry works like an auction bid: if it later beats the market’s hidden price, you pay the market price — not your number. Reporting your true maximum is always your best move.

🏡 Open House Interest:
You may indicate whether you are interested in attending the property’s open house.

⏱ No Timer:
There is no countdown timer during Phase 1. Take your time and review each property carefully.
