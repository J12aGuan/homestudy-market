Phase 1

Property Valuation Stage

In this initial phase, you will evaluate four housing listings from your chosen market type, shown side by side. Each house profile lists its bedrooms and bathrooms, property type, walkability or transit access, cost of living, and school district rating.

  • Concealed Market Conditions: The property's market asking price is intentionally hidden during this baseline stage.
  • Attending an Open House: For each property, you have the choice to click the Attend Open House toggle to indicate whether or not you want to attend.
  • Valuation Entry: For each house profile, you must declare the absolute maximum baseline price you would personally be willing to pay.
  • Your Best Strategy — Report Honestly: Think of your entry as an auction bid. If it beats the market's hidden price, you pay the market price — not your number. Overstating can leave you paying more than the house is worth to you; understating can cost you the house. Reporting your true maximum is always your best move.
  • No Active Timers: There are no round countdown limits in Phase 1. Take your time to carefully review each house configuration.
