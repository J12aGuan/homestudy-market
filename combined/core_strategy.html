Your Primary Target

You are restricted to purchasing exactly ONE home.

A successful bid ends Phase 2 immediately — the house you win is the house you buy. Bidding higher makes success more likely, but if you win you pay the seller's price, so bid what each house is truly worth to you.

Waiting is not free: after an unsuccessful round a house can leave the market, and you have a limited number of chances to see new houses. Balance how much you bid against the risk of losing the house you want. You may exit the market at any time without purchasing.
