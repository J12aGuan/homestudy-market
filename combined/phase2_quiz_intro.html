💡 Phase 2 Hints

🏠 Bidding Market:
In Phase 2, you see four houses from your market type, each with a visible reference price.

💵 Reference Price:
The reference price is a benchmark to guide your bid. The seller's actual price stays hidden.

🎯 Winning a Bid:
If your bid meets the seller's hidden price, you purchase the house and pay the seller's price — not your bid. A successful purchase ends Phase 2 immediately.

🔁 Bidding Rounds:
If a bid is unsuccessful, you can revise it and try again on the same house — up to 4 rounds in total.

⚠️ Market Scarcity:
After an unsuccessful round, a house may be sold to someone else and disappear from the market.

🔄 Your Options:
At any point you can bid on a house, ask to see a different set of four houses (up to 4 times), or exit the home purchasing market entirely.
