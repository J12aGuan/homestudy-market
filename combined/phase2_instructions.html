Phase 2

Housing Bidding Market

In Phase 2 you enter a live bidding market. You will see four houses, each with a reference price to guide your decisions. Your goal is to try to purchase one house by bidding.

  • Reference Prices: Every house displays a reference price. Use it as a benchmark when deciding how much to bid.
  • Placing Bids: Choose a house and enter a bid. If your bid meets the seller's price, the purchase succeeds and Phase 2 ends. If not, you may bid again on the same house — up to 4 rounds in total.
  • Houses Can Leave the Market: After an unsuccessful round, the house may be sold to someone else and disappear before you can bid again.
  • Your Options: At any point you can bid on a house, ask to see a different set of four houses (up to 4 times), or exit the home purchasing market entirely.
