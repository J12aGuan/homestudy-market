BEFORE YOU BEGIN
What is your maximum price for a house?
You will see properties three at a time, over four rounds. For every property we ask you one question: the most you would pay for it.
💡 What “maximum price” means
Your maximum price is the highest price you would still be happy to pay to own that house. At exactly that price, buying the house and walking away from it feel about equally good to you. One dollar more, and you would rather walk away.
How to decide on a number
Picture living there
Read the bedrooms, bathrooms, size, and neighborhood details, and imagine the house as your home. Ask what that home is worth to you.
Your value, not the market’s
We are not asking what you think the house would sell for, or what you hope to pay. We are asking what it is worth to you.
Answer for each house
Houses differ from round to round. Give each one its own number rather than repeating your last answer.
There are no right or wrong answers, and no house prices are shown — the number you give is your own valuation. Please answer as honestly as you can: the study depends on your true values, not on strategic guesses.
